# Q5 — Bulk Discounts

- Print items receive a 10% discount when 10 or more copies are purchased.
- Electronic resources charge full price for the first 50 seats and half price for every additional seat.
- Hardcover pricing retains the Q4 surcharge before applying the bulk discount.

## Files
- `Book.cpp`
- `Journal.cpp`
- `ElectronicResource.cpp`
- `test_main.cpp`
- `demo.cpp`

## Expected results
- 10 paperback copies at 100.00 each: 900.00
- 10 hardcover copies at 100.00 each: 1080.00
- 60 electronic seats at 10.00 each plus a 100.00 platform fee: 650.00
